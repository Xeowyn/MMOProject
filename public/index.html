Nearby Adventurers

    Mining

    Every ore node you've unlocked, in one place — no travel needed. Discovering a mine's location on the Overworld map unlocks its node here. Click a node to mine it; click the active node again to stop.

    Fishing

    Every fishing spot you've unlocked, in one place — no travel needed. Discovering a spot's location on the Overworld map unlocks it here. Click a spot to fish it; click the active spot again to stop.

    Optional: click the instant a fish bites for a bonus catch. Ignore it and fishing still works exactly like any other task.
